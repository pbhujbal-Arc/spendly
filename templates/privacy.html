Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

1. Data We Collect

To provide the service, Spendly collects only the information you give us:

  • Account details, such as your name, email address, and password.
  • Expense records you enter, including amount, category, date, and description.
  • Basic technical information, such as login times, used to keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to run Spendly for you. This includes:

  • Showing your expenses, category breakdowns, and monthly summaries.
  • Signing you in and protecting your account.
  • Contacting you about important changes to the service.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secured database. Passwords are stored in hashed form and are never kept in plain text.

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your expense records are permanently removed.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal or financial data with third parties. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive basic technical information such as your IP address when the page loads.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to request a copy or deletion of your data, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.
